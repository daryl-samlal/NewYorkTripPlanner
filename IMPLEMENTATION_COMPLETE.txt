HOW TO USE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: EDIT YOUR JSONBIN DATA
   Go to: https://jsonbin.io/
   Find: Your NYC trip plan
   Locate: "confirmed" object

STEP 2: ADD TRANSPORTS ARRAY
   Add this to "confirmed":
   
   "transports": [
     {
       "type": "bus",
       "from": "JFK Airport",
       "to": "Times Square",
       "departureTime": "17:00",
       "arrivalTime": "18:30",
       "bookingRef": "BUS-NYC-001",
       "price": "15"
     }
   ]

STEP 3: SAVE & RELOAD
   Click: Save button in JSONBin
   Reload: Your app in browser
   See: Transportation card updated!

WHAT'S DIFFERENT FROM FLIGHTS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Flights (Existing):
   • Stored in: confirmed.flights
   • Display: Individual flight cards
   • Fields: flight, from, to, departure, arrival

Transports (New):
   • Stored in: confirmed.transports
   • Display: Individual transport cards
   • Fields: type, from, to, departureTime, arrivalTime, bookingRef, price
   • Both combined in: Transportation Card
   • Status badge: Shows combined total
